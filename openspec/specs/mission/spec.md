# Mission Module

This spec covers all capabilities of the Mission module.

## Capabilities

- [mission-people](./mission-people/) — Person entity, CRUD, search, and member status tracking
- [mission-bible-studies](./mission-bible-studies/) — Bible study progress and instructor assignment
- [mission-missionary-pairs](./mission-missionary-pairs/) — Missionary teams (formerly pairs) management
- [mission-visits](./mission-visits/) — Visit cases with attempt tracking per person
- [mission-rescue-members](./mission-rescue-members/) — Rescue follow-up for lapsed members
- [mission-catalogs](./mission-catalogs/) — Shared catalogs like Sabbath School classes
- [mission-module-access](./mission-module-access/) — Role-based access control for the mission module
